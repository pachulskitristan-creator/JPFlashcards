Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026 · Applies to the JP Flashcards iOS and Android app

This explains what JP Flashcards collects, why, and how you can delete it. The short version: you can use the entire app as a guest with nothing leaving your phone; creating an account only adds an email address and a backup copy of your study progress.

What we collect Guest vs. account How we use it Who we share it with Subscriptions Retention & deletion Children Your rights Changes Contact

§1What we collect

Data
Why
Email address
Only if you create an account. Used to sign you in and to identify your subscription.
Password
Only if you create an account. Stored hashed by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see it in plain text.
Study progress
Which words you've reviewed, your spaced-repetition schedule, XP, streak, and any custom vocabulary or tags you add. Lives on your device; copied to our servers only if you're signed in, so it can follow you to a new device.
Purchase / subscription status
Whether you hold an active Pro subscription, and which product. Payment itself is handled entirely by Apple or Google — we never see your card details.
What we don't collect: no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, no location, no contacts, no microphone or camera access. The app's text-to-speech reads words aloud using your device's own speech engine — nothing is recorded or sent anywhere for that.

§2Guest vs. account

JP Flashcards works fully as a guest. In guest mode, everything above except email/password stays on your device only, in local storage — we never receive it.

Creating an account is optional and adds two things: an email address for sign-in, and a backup copy of your study progress on our servers, so it survives a reinstall or carries over to a new phone.

§3How we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your account secure.
  • To back up and restore your study progress across devices.
  • To check whether you hold an active Pro subscription, so the app can unlock the right content.
  • To respond if you email us for support.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising — we don't run any.

§4Who we share it with

Three services process data on our behalf, each only for the narrow purpose below:

Service
What it handles
Supabase
Hosts your account and, if you're signed in, your synced study progress. Their own privacy practices apply to how they store this: supabase.com/privacy.
RevenueCat
Tracks which subscription you hold, linked to your account so it follows you across devices. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
Apple / Google
Process the actual subscription payment through the App Store or Play Store. We never receive your payment details.

§5Subscriptions

If you buy a JP Flashcards Pro subscription, Apple or Google (whichever store you bought it through) is the merchant of record. We only receive confirmation of your entitlement — active or not — never your card number or billing address.

§6Retention & deletion

We keep your account and synced progress for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings inside the app — this permanently removes your email, password, and cloud-synced progress from our servers immediately. Local, on-device data is removed by uninstalling the app.

§7Children

JP Flashcards is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

§8Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask us to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data we hold about you — most of this you can already do yourself in-app (account deletion is self-service, see §6). For anything else, email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

§9Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we'll update the effective date above and, where required, notify you in-app before it takes effect.

§10Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: jpflashcards.support@icloud.com.
