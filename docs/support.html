Common questions

My purchase didn't unlock Pro — what do I do?

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't unlock, email us with the email address on your account and the date you purchased.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions) — not from inside the app, per each store's own requirements.

How do I delete my account and data?

Settings → Delete Account, inside the app. This is immediate and permanent, and removes your synced study progress along with your account. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what gets deleted.

My study progress didn't carry over to my new phone.

Progress only syncs if you're signed in to an account on both devices. If you were using guest mode on your old phone, that data lived only on that device and unfortunately can't be recovered.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

Email us — screenshots and steps to reproduce help a lot if it's a bug.
